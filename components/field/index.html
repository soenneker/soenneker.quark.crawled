Field

Combine labels, controls, and help text to compose accessible form fields and grouped inputs.

Payment Method

All transactions are secure and encrypted

Enter your 16-digit card number

Billing Address

The billing address associated with your payment method

																			<Div Width="Width.IsFull" MaxWidth="MaxWidth.IsMd">     <form>         <FieldGroup>             <FieldSet>

Input

Choose a unique username for your account.

Must be at least 8 characters long.

																			<FieldSet Width="Width.IsFull" MaxWidth="MaxWidth.IsXs">     <Field>         <FieldLabel For="field-username">Username</FieldLabel>         <Input Id="field-username" Placeholder="shadcn" />

Textarea

Share your thoughts about our service.

																			<FieldSet Width="Width.IsFull" MaxWidth="MaxWidth.IsXs">     <FieldGroup>         <Field>             <FieldLabel For="feedback">Feedback</FieldLabel>

Select

Select your department or area of work.

																			<Field MaxWidth="MaxWidth.IsXs">     <FieldLabel For="field-department">Department</FieldLabel>     <Select TValue="string" SelectedValue="@("")">         <SelectTrigger Id="field-department">

Slider

Set your budget range ($200 - 800).

																			<Field MaxWidth="MaxWidth.IsXs">     <FieldLabel For="field-price-range">Price Range</FieldLabel>     <Slider Id="field-price-range" Min="0" Max="1000" Step="50" Values="@([200d, 800d])" />     <FieldDescription>Set your budget range ($200 - 800).</FieldDescription>

Fieldset

Address Information

We need your address to deliver your order.

																			<FieldSet MaxWidth="MaxWidth.IsSm">     <FieldLegend>Address Information</FieldLegend>     <FieldDescription>We need your address to deliver your order.</FieldDescription>     <FieldGroup>

Checkbox

Show these items on the desktop

Select the items you want to show on the desktop.

Your Desktop & Documents folders are being synced with iCloud Drive. You can access them from other devices.

																			<FieldGroup MaxWidth="MaxWidth.IsXs">     <FieldSet>         <FieldLegend Variant="label">Show these items on the desktop</FieldLegend>         <FieldDescription>Select the items you want to show on the desktop.</FieldDescription>

Radio

Subscription Plan

Yearly and lifetime plans offer significant savings.

																			<FieldSet MaxWidth="MaxWidth.IsXs">     <FieldLegend Variant="label">Subscription Plan</FieldLegend>     <FieldDescription>Yearly and lifetime plans offer significant savings.</FieldDescription>     <RadioGroup Name="field-subscription-plan" Value="yearly">

Switch

																			<Field Orientation="@Orientation.Horizontal" MaxWidth="MaxWidth.IsSm">     <FieldLabel For="field-mfa">Multi-factor authentication</FieldLabel>     <Switch Id="field-mfa" /> </Field>

Choice Card

Wrap Field components inside FieldLabel to create selectable field groups. This works with RadioItem, Checkbox and Switch components.
Compute Environment

Select the compute environment for your cluster.

																			<FieldSet MaxWidth="MaxWidth.IsXs">     <FieldLegend Variant="label">Compute Environment</FieldLegend>     <FieldDescription>Select the compute environment for your cluster.</FieldDescription>     <RadioGroup Name="field-compute-environment" Value="kubernetes" Display="Display.Grid" Gap="Gap.Is3">

Field Group

Stack Field components with FieldGroup. Add FieldSeparator to divide them.
Responses

Get notified when ChatGPT responds to requests that take time, like research or image generation.

Tasks

Get notified when tasks you've created have updates. Manage tasks 

																			<FieldGroup MaxWidth="MaxWidth.IsXs">     <FieldSet>         <FieldContent>             <FieldTitle>Responses</FieldTitle>

RTL

Horizontal Layout

Vertical fields stack label, control, and helper text. Set Orientation to Orientation.Horizontal on Field to align the label and control side-by-side. Pair with FieldContent to keep descriptions aligned.
