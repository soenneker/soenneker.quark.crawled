Recipe example

Chocolate chip cookies steps.

Chocolate Chip Cookies

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C)
  2. Mix dry ingredients - In a bowl, combine flour, baking soda, and salt
  3. Cream butter and sugars - Beat butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until fluffy
  4. Add eggs and vanilla - Beat in eggs one at a time, then vanilla
  5. Combine wet and dry - Gradually blend in flour mixture
  6. Add chocolate chips - Stir in chocolate chips
  7. Bake - Drop rounded tablespoons onto ungreased cookie sheets and bake for 9-11 minutes

OrderedList features

Overview of components and options.

OrderedList Features

The OrderedList and OrderedListItem components provide:

  • OrderedList: Main container for numbered lists
  • OrderedListItem: Individual numbered list items
  • Nesting: Support for nested numbered lists
  • Numbering Styles: Various list-style-type options
  • Starting Numbers: Custom start values
  • Styling: Custom CSS classes and styles
  • Interactive Content: Buttons, forms, and other components
  • Accessibility: Proper semantic HTML structure
